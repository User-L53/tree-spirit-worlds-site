About

We tell stories
and build worlds.

Tree Spirit Worlds is an independent storytelling and world-building studio. We create stories that take shape across literature, digital experiences, sound, and physical objects.

Who we are

Storytellers.

For us, a story does not have to end at the edge of a page. It can become a place to enter, an environment to move through, something to hear, or an object to hold.

World-builders.

Tree Spirit Worlds brings these forms together by building worlds in which stories can take different shapes. Individual works can become worlds within it.

What we create

Read

Illustrated books, ebooks, and literary works.

Enter

Interactive digital stories and narrative experiences.

Listen

Narrated stories, audio works, and sound-led experiences.

Hold

Printed editions and physical objects that extend a story beyond the screen or page.

Why we create

We create spaces where other possibilities may be encountered.

A story can leave room for a different way of seeing, remembering, or imagining what comes next.

Not answers.
Possibilities.
