Who we are

An independent, digital-first creative world.

Tree Spirit Worlds is the master world from which individual literary works, illustrated stories, interactive experiences, audio, and physical cultural objects can emerge. Rooty is its psyche, voice, and guide.

Why this exists

To make room for another way of seeing.

It began from the idea of creating a projected world of idealism: a space in which inner experience can take spatial form, change is part of the environment, and time need not behave as a line.

Our mission

To create spaces for other possibilities of being.

Tree Spirit Worlds creates spaces in which people can temporarily step outside the reality they habitually inhabit and encounter other possibilities of being, remembering, changing, and experiencing time.
