The first work

The Internal Clock

In a world where thoughts become places, five-year-old Sophie has all the time in the world.

She lives among ancient trees, rivers, and mountains with Rooty—the strange, gentle psyche of the world around her. Here, anger can become a mountain. A thought can change the weather. And time, Rooty tells her, does not move the way it does elsewhere.

But there is something Sophie cannot understand. The woman she sometimes sees in the water keeps getting older. Sophie does not.

As the world begins to reveal what it has been holding, Sophie is drawn toward a question she has carried since she was very small: if everything changes, does that mean everything we love must eventually disappear?

An illustrated literary journey through childhood, memory, time, and the strange ways we try to detach from a past that still shapes us.

About the studio

Not answers.
Possibilities.

Tree Spirit Worlds is an independent creative world-building and narrative studio. We create contemplative stories, illustrated publications, interactive experiences, audio works, and cultural objects.

Each work begins with a simple question: what if an inner experience could become a place—somewhere you could walk through, look at differently, and carry home in another form?
