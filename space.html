The space between

Tree Spirit Worlds exists in the space between what we have experienced and who we believe ourselves to be.

Time as space

Here, what has passed does not have to disappear behind us. It can remain somewhere we can return to.

An experience can become a place. A memory can become an object. Something once fixed can be touched, moved, and imagined differently.

Time exists, but does not necessarily behave as a line.

Play / Transformation

Here, we can play with what we have carried—almost the way children play with earth.

Pick it up. Move it. Give it another shape. Leave it where it is. Return to it later.

An experience.

For a moment, you can step outside the reality through which you usually recognize yourself.

Your name, your history, your expectations, your memories—none of them need to disappear. They simply do not have to explain you here.

Our stories make space between what has happened and what it has come to mean.
